About

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Data Science at Umm Al-Qura University in Makkah, with more than 15 years of experience in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the University of East Anglia in 2017, with a thesis on clustering ensemble methods, and my MSc in Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining from the same university with distinction. My bachelor's degree is from Umm Al-Qura University.

Alongside teaching and research, I have served as Acting Dean of the Institute of Manuscripts and Islamic Heritage Revival, and I was a visiting researcher at Northumbria University. I am a Certified Artificial Intelligence Scientist (CAIS).
